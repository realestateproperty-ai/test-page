Removal of Trustee (Standard)

Use this form if a trustee is being removed for reasons such as:

Change in plan management
Business restructuring
Divorce or separation
Voluntary resignation

This document creates a formal resolution removing the trustee in accordance with the plan's governing documents and authorizes the necessary updates to the plan records.

Form 2

Removal of Trustee Due to Death

Use this form if a trustee has passed away.

This version includes additional language confirming the trustee's death and formally documenting their removal from the plan while ensuring compliance with the plan's provisions.

Important Considerations
